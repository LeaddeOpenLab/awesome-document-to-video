# How to turn a PDF into a training video

A reusable workflow for converting a policy, handbook, product manual, or learning document into a concise training video without simply reading the PDF aloud.

**Best for:** learning and development, customer education, compliance, product training, and internal communications.

## Before you start

Confirm the audience, observable learning outcome, authoritative pages, and review owner. Remove passwords, personal information, customer data, and internal material that should not be uploaded to a third-party service.

## 1. Create a learning brief

```text
Source document: [title and version]
Audience: [role, prior knowledge, region]
Learning outcome: After watching, the viewer can [observable action].
Required facts: [facts that must remain exact]
Optional context: [details that can be summarized]
Required warning or disclaimer: [text]
Target duration: [minutes]
Tone: [clear, formal, reassuring, technical]
Call to action: [quiz, confirmation, next task, contact]
```

## 2. Create a video-ready outline

```text
Analyze the supplied document as source material for a training video.

Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Learning outcome: [OUTCOME]
Target duration: [DURATION]

For each scene provide one learning point, narration of no more than
65 spoken words, the source page or section, a visual treatment, and
any statement requiring subject-matter review.

Do not invent policies, figures, steps, or examples. Mark missing
information as [REVIEW REQUIRED]. End with a three-point recap and
one next action.
```

## 3. Review a short draft

Generate the first two or three scenes before the complete video. Check that every factual statement traces to the PDF, on-screen text is readable, each scene communicates one idea, and the viewer is told what to do next.

With [Leadde's AI Video Creator](https://leadde.ai/features/ai-video-creator?utm_source=github&utm_medium=workflow&utm_campaign=pdf-training), you can upload a supported document, choose the target language, tone, detail level, and template, then review the generated script and scenes before export.

## Common failures

| Failure | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| The video reads the PDF verbatim | Rewrite around one outcome and one point per scene |
| Important exceptions disappear | List mandatory facts and warnings first |
| Too much text appears on screen | Show keywords; keep explanation in narration |
| Localization changes meaning | Approve the source-language script first |
| The video is too long | Move reference detail into a companion document |

## Final quality gate

- [ ] Source title and version recorded
- [ ] Critical claims traced to source pages
- [ ] Subject-matter owner approved the script
- [ ] Names, numbers, and pronunciation checked
- [ ] Captions and on-screen text checked
- [ ] Localized versions reviewed by a competent speaker
