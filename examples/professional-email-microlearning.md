## Learning brief

Turn a familiar workplace topic into a short employee-enablement lesson. The public template focuses on:

- structuring a professional email;
- writing a useful subject line;
- stating the purpose clearly;
- avoiding common communication mistakes.

## Why this is a useful pattern

The subject is narrow, behavior-focused, and relevant across departments. The same structure can be reused for short modules such as meeting etiquette, escalation rules, customer handoffs, security reminders, or expense-policy basics.

## Reusable source outline

```text
Audience: [team or role]
Behavior to improve: [one observable workplace behavior]
Why it matters: [business or learner consequence]
Teach: [three to five rules]
Show: [one weak example and one improved example]
Close with: [one action the learner should take]
Target format: short avatar-led microlearning video
```

The outline above is a repository adaptation, not the undisclosed prompt used to create the official template.
