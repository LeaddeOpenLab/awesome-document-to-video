This example shows the production path most relevant to L&D, HR, compliance, and operations teams: upload an existing document, review the generated teaching script, turn it into short scenes, choose the presenter and voice, then export the module.

![Upload a document in Leadde](https://static.leadde.ai/uploads/Screenshot_2026_05_08_at_9_12_47_AM_6ad4af5cc0.png)

## Publicly demonstrated workflow

1. Upload a handbook, policy, SOP, product guide, course handout, or other structured document.
2. Review the generated script for teaching clarity instead of simply reading the source word for word.
3. Break the lesson into focused scenes, with one idea, step, or decision in each scene.
4. Choose a presenter and voice, then adjust pacing and visual emphasis.
5. Review the finished module and export or share it through the team's existing learning channel.

## Why this example fits the repository

The differentiator is not a single visual prompt. It is the full path from business source material to a reviewable training asset. That makes this a useful starting point for:

- employee onboarding;
- SOP and compliance training;
- customer education;
- product and process documentation.
