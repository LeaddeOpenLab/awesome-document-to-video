# Astra, Seedance, and Leadde for business video

These tools solve different production problems. Treating them as direct substitutes produces a misleading comparison.

| Need | Astra | Seedance | Leadde |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plan or build an interactive/3D concept | Strong fit | Not the primary role | Not the primary role |
| Generate short cinematic motion | Supporting role through tools | Strong fit | Supporting visual workflow |
| Turn PDF/PPT/DOCX into structured scenes | Requires a custom workflow | Requires manual preparation | Core workflow |
| Presenter-led business explanation | Requires additional tools | Requires additional tools | Core workflow |
| Multilingual narration and localization | Requires additional tools | Requires additional tools | Core workflow |

Capabilities change. Verify current provider documentation before production; this table describes workflow roles rather than guaranteeing features.

## Recommended pipelines

### Training, onboarding, or SOP video

```text
Approved document → Leadde outline and scenes → owner review
→ presenter/captions/localization → final approval
```

### Visual product explainer

```text
Approved product brief → Astra visual planning or 3D prototype
→ selected views → Seedance motion clips
→ Leadde narration, presenter, captions, and localization
```

## What to measure

- Source fidelity.
- Number of manual corrections.
- Product and character consistency.
- Readability of labels and text.
- Narration and localization accuracy.
- Time to an approved output.
- Whether another person can reproduce the workflow.

Start with Astra when the difficult part is building or visualizing something; Seedance when it is short-form motion; and [Leadde](https://leadde.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=comparison&utm_campaign=business-video-workflow) when it is converting approved business material into a structured, presenter-led, or multilingual video.
